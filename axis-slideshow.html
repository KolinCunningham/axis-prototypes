Idea 1 · Protect the firm

Portfolio-Wide Lodgement Risk Triage

Problem: a missed deadline gets noticed client-by-client, often after it's already late.
What it does: ranks every client in the book by risk, using lodgement history, document completeness and complexity, tells partners who to chase this week.

Idea 2 · Grow the firm

Family Trust & Structuring Growth Options

Problem: structuring advice is usually reactive, clients don't know what they don't know.
What it does: enter a client's situation, get the 2 to 5 most relevant structuring options with a plain-English reason each applies.

Idea 3 · Grow the firm

Cashflow Gap & Upsell Timing

Problem: the best moment to offer extra services is when a client's cashflow shifts, nobody's watching for it between annual reviews.
What it does: visualises 12-month cashflow per client, flags unusual gaps or buildups, and suggests the talking point.

Idea 4 · Grow the firm

Unbilled Work / Scope-Creep Detector

Problem: staff quietly do more for a client than the engagement covers, and none of it gets invoiced.
What it does: compares estimated work effort against what's invoiced, surfaces a dollar "unbilled gap" per client, worst offenders first.

Bonus · Internal ops, not client-facing

Staff Capacity & Document Chase-Up

Problem: post-merger, two teams with different skills run EOFY by hand, matching lodgement deadlines to staff capacity, and chasing outstanding client documents, both tracked in spreadsheets and memory.
What it does: one dashboard flags overloaded staff with a rebalancing suggestion, and tracks documents received versus outstanding per client.
