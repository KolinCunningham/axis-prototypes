Internal Ops & Capacity Logistics

Internal view only, not something a client would ever see. Since Carollo & Co joined AXIS, the tax team and the SMSF team run under one roof but still plan their EOFY crunch by memory and separate spreadsheets. This pulls both into one screen: who is overloaded right now, and which clients are about to go late purely because paperwork has not landed yet.

Staff Capacity Load Balancer

Current workload against standard fortnight capacity, across both merged teams. A card turns red when someone is booked over capacity, gold when they are right at the line, and green when they have room to take on more.

Document Chase-Up Tracker

Every client still in scope for this EOFY cycle, with documents received against what is still outstanding. Click a row to see exactly what is missing. Clients flagged at risk are on track to lodge late for no reason other than paperwork.
